# 0005. The project is dual-licensed: AGPLv3 open edition plus a separate commercial license

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-27 (records a decision made at project inception)
- **Deciders:** Michael (owner)
- **Topics:** licensing, commercial

## Context

The project is open source but needs a sustainable commercial model, and the owner must remain the sole source of non-AGPL rights.

## Decision

1. The open edition is **AGPLv3**.
2. Any commercial use (hosted services, proprietary derivatives, MSP offerings, closed products) requires a **separate non-AGPL license purchased from the owner**, who is the sole legal source of non-AGPL rights.
3. No change may let third parties offer competing commercial services without a commercial license from the owner.

## Consequences

- Easier: open distribution with a protected commercial boundary.
- Harder: contributions require the CLA; every change is weighed for commercial/licensing impact.
- Now a rule: preserve the dual-license model in all licensing-related work; do not weaken the commercial boundary.
