# Commercial Licensing for PosternProxy

PosternProxy offers both a free open-source edition and commercial licenses.

## Open Source Edition (AGPLv3)
The core PosternProxy software is available under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3)](../LICENSE).

This provides full access to the single-binary controller, multi-server agent architecture, SSH provisioning, audit logging, config history/rollback, and all other features for individuals, homelabbers, and organizations comfortable with AGPL terms.

## Commercial / Enterprise Licenses
For organizations that cannot or prefer not to use AGPLv3, we offer separate commercial licenses directly from the project owner (the copyright holder).

These licenses remove the AGPL copyleft obligations and grant additional rights, including:

- Use in proprietary or closed-source software and products
- Offering PosternProxy (or derivatives) as a hosted service or SaaS
- Embedding in commercial appliances or managed services
- Redistribution under terms of your choice
- Priority support, SLAs, and custom development options

### Why Commercial Licensing?
Many enterprises have policies against AGPL or need the flexibility to build proprietary solutions around the software. By owning the copyright, the project owner is the **sole legal provider** of non-AGPL rights. This model protects the open-source community while giving paying customers exactly the legal certainty they require.

### Available Tiers (Examples)
- **Enterprise License**: Core non-AGPL rights for internal and limited commercial use.
- **Fleet / MSP License**: Rights for managing multiple customer environments and offering as a managed service.
- **Hosted Control Plane License**: Rights to operate a hosted version of the PosternProxy controller.
- **Support & Maintenance**: Annual support contracts with SLAs, updates, and security patches.
- **Custom / OEM**: White-labeling, branding removal, or deep integration work.

Pricing is customized based on deployment size, number of servers/agents, and usage model. Contact us for a quote.
